I'm excited to announce that the City Council [has approved](https://lims.minneapolismn.gov/File/2024-00045) my appointment to the [Minneapolis Advisory Committee on People with Disabilities](https://lims.minneapolismn.gov/Boards/macopd) for [Ward 2](https://www.minneapolismn.gov/government/city-council/members/ward-2/about-ward-2/), which includes the University of Minnesota. I'm honored to take on this role.

The Committee advises the Mayor and City Council on disability issues, promoting accessibility and inclusivity in the city. In my work and through my experiences, I've seen firsthand the challenges (and triumphs) within the disability community. This appointment is an opportunity to contribute more significantly to positive change.

I look forward to contributing to meaningful change and ensuring the needs and voices of the disability community are heard. A big thank you to Mayor Frey for this opportunity.
