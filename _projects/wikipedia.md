This page contains a list of my contributions to Wikipedia. I have been editing Wikipedia since 2015, and I have made over 1,000 edits. I have created several articles and have made many improvements to existing articles. I have also participated in various WikiProjects and have helped to improve the quality of articles in a wide range of subject areas. Many of my Commons contributions are found under [Visualizations](/visualizations).

My username on Wikipedia is [Petermgrund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/User:Petermgrund).

A <i class="fa-solid fa-user-pen xs"></i> icon indicates that I created the article or am the primary contributor.
